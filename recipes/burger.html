MSG Smash Burger

A delicious and juicy burger that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices of cheese (optional)
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles for toppings

Instructions

  1. Preheat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat.
  2. Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape them into patties
  3. Season the patties with salt and pepper on both sides.
  4. Place the patties on the skillet or grill and cook for 3-4 minutes on each side for medium doneness, or until desired doneness is reached.
  5. If using cheese, place a slice on each patty during the last minute of cooking to melt.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns on the skillet or grill for 1-2 minutes until lightly browned.
  7. Assemble the burgers by placing the cooked patties on the bottom half of the buns, adding your desired toppings, and covering with the top half of the buns.
  8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
